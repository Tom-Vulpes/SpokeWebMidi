How to record a sound

  1. Press and hold the red button.
  2. Make your sound into the microphone.
  3. Let go when you've finished. You'll hear it straight away.
  4. Don't like it? Hold the button again to record a new one.

Connect your rocket

  1. Plug the SPOKE board into the computer with the USB cable.
  2. Clip the LEFT fin to Pad 1, BOOST to Pad 11 and the RIGHT fin to Pad 26.
  3. Press Connect my rocket. If the computer asks, press Allow.
  4. Touch each tape patch. Its box below should light up yellow and get a tick.
